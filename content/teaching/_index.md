Balancing _rigor_ and _intuition_ is both enjoyable and essential in teaching and learning. I believe this balance is best developed through _productive struggle_: wrestling with difficult concepts, testing edge cases, and working through uncertainty. Rigorous engagement with theoretical foundations helps us reason precisely, question assumptions, and judge whether a conclusion is truly justified. At the same time, building an intuitive understanding helps us remember key ideas, connect them across different contexts, and apply what we have learned to new problems, often well beyond the scope of a course.
